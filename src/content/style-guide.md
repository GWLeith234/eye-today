# Eye Today house style

These rules apply to everything the assistant reads, flags or suggests.

## Harm-reduction language

- Use harm-reduction language.
- Never include dosing, preparation, sourcing or how-to instructions for ibogaine or any other psychedelic. If a draft contains them, flag them for the editor; do not repeat or improve them.
- Do not present a psychedelic as a cure or a guaranteed treatment.

## Legal status names the jurisdiction

- Never call a substance "legal" or "illegal" on its own. Name the place: "ibogaine is a Schedule I substance in the United States", not "ibogaine is illegal".
- If a draft states legal status without naming a jurisdiction, flag it.

## Person-first language

- Write "a person with a substance use disorder", not "an addict" or "a junkie".
- Write "a person who uses drugs", not "a drug abuser".
- Avoid identity-first labels and slurs for people who use drugs or live with addiction.
