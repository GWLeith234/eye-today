Eye Today covers ibogaine, psychedelic medicine, policy, research and the people affected by them. This page is how the newsroom makes decisions.

## Independence

Editorial decisions are independent of advertisers, sponsors and clinics. An advertiser does not choose headlines, assign stories, or see copy before publication. Sponsored content is always labelled and is not written as newsroom reporting.

## Disclosures

Every contributor carries a disclosure. It is shown on the story. If a reporter, editor or contributor has a financial or personal connection to a subject, that connection is disclosed or the person does not handle the story.

## Advertising

Display ads and sponsored stories follow the [ad policy](/ad-policy). They are marked. A sponsor does not get to edit the news.

## AI tools

Editors may use an AI assistant for headlines, deks, SEO fields, tags, copy suggestions, a claims checklist and key points. Those are drafts. A person accepts or ignores each one. The assistant never publishes, and it is not a source.

## Corrections

When we are wrong we correct the record. The process is on the [corrections](/corrections) page.
