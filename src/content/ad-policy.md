# Eye Today ad policy

Editors check every creative against this page before approving it. A pending or rejected creative never serves.

## What a creative may contain

- Text with `p`, `br`, `strong`, `em`, `a` and `img` only. Anything else is removed when the creative is saved.
- Links must be `https`. They open in a new tab and are marked `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`.
- Images must be `https` or come from the site's own media library, with alt text.
- No script, iframe, form, tracking pixel or event handler. These are stripped, and a creative with nothing left is not saved.

## What is not accepted

- Dosing instructions for ibogaine or any other psychedelic or controlled substance.
- Offers to buy, sell or source a controlled substance.
- Cure claims, guaranteed outcomes or "treats addiction" claims that are not clearly labelled and sourced. An unlabeled claim that a substance or treatment cures a condition is rejected.
- Content that is not clearly an advertisement, or that imitates Eye Today reporting.
- Language that shames people who use drugs. Use person-first wording.

## Categories

Every creative has one category: clinic, research, advocacy, events or other.

## Reviewing

Approve only when the destination page and the creative both meet this policy. Reject anything you are unsure about; the advertiser can send a new creative.
