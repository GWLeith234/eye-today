When a story is wrong, we correct it. We would rather fix a fact than defend it.

## How to tell us

Use the [contact](/contact) page. Include the story link, the sentence you believe is wrong, and the source that supports the correction. We read requests from readers, sources and the people named in a story.

## What we do

An editor checks the request against the original reporting. If we got a fact wrong, we change the story and add a note that says what changed and when. A small typo that does not change meaning may be fixed without a note.

We do not remove a story because someone dislikes a fair report. We do correct names, titles, numbers, dates and quotations when we have them wrong.

## Timing

We aim to acknowledge a request within two business days and to publish a correction as soon as we have confirmed it.
