Eye Today runs a paid job board and a classifieds section for people working in addiction treatment, ibogaine and psychedelic care, research, harm reduction, training and related services. An editor reads every posting before it goes live. Paying for a posting does not guarantee publication.

## What you can post

- **Jobs:** paid and volunteer roles at clinics, research groups, harm-reduction services, non-profits, publishers and suppliers to the field.
- **Classifieds:** training and courses, professional services, retreats and integration programmes, and other offers relevant to practitioners.

## What we do not publish

- Anything that sells, supplies, sources or gives away ibogaine, psilocybin or any other controlled or unapproved substance, or plant material containing them.
- Dosing, preparation or "how to" instructions.
- Claims that a substance or service cures, treats or prevents a condition, unless you name the published source. Editors may ask you to remove a claim.
- Treatment offered where it is illegal, or by people who cannot lawfully provide it.
- Pyramid schemes, investment offers, paid "ambassador" roles and unpaid work that should be paid.
- Discrimination that breaks the law where the role is based.
- Misleading pay, titles, locations or organisation names.

## Applying

Applications and enquiries go straight to the link or email address you give. Eye Today does not collect applications, screen applicants or take part in hiring.

## Payment, review and expiry

- A posting runs for the 30 or 60 days you paid for, counted from the day an editor approves it, or until its closing date if that comes first. It then comes down automatically.
- If an editor rejects your posting, we tell you why. You can fix it and send it back, or ask for a refund.
- Editing a live posting sends it back to an editor. It stays off the board until it is approved again, and the clock keeps running.
- You can renew a posting before or after it ends. An approved posting whose text has not changed goes straight back up for the time you renew.

## Your details

We keep your account email to contact you about your posting. It is never published. Payment is handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details.
