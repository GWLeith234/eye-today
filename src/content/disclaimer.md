Eye Today publishes information. It is not medical advice and it is not legal advice.

Ibogaine and other psychedelics can cause serious harm, including heart complications and death. They are illegal in many places. Nothing we publish is an instruction to obtain, possess or take a substance, and nothing here is a recommendation to start, stop or change a treatment.

Laws differ by country, province and state. A practice described in a story may be a crime where you are. If you need legal advice, talk to a lawyer who is licensed where you live.

If you need medical care, talk to a qualified clinician who can see you. Do not use this website as a substitute for that.
