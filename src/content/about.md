Eye Today is an independent newsroom covering ibogaine, psychedelic medicine, and the people, policy and science around them.

We write for readers who want sourced reporting rather than hype. Stories are reported and edited by people. Advertising and sponsorship do not decide what we cover. The [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) says how that works, and the [disclaimer](/disclaimer) says what this site is not.

## Who runs Eye Today

**Jeff Davis, Editor and Publisher.** Jeff is a journalist by training. He reported from Ottawa's Parliamentary Press Gallery for Postmedia, covering defence, foreign affairs, intelligence and public safety, and his work has appeared in the National Post, The Globe and Mail, the Saskatoon StarPhoenix and The Canadian Press. He spent several years with Canada's Department of Foreign Affairs in Ottawa and Washington, D.C., and as an Aga Khan Foundation Canada media fellow worked with Nation Media Group in Nairobi, Kenya. He has also built businesses, including a social enterprise that employs leatherworkers in Kenya, and spent nearly a decade in sales leadership at Vendasta. Jeff holds a degree in journalism and political science from Carleton University and lives in Saskatoon.

Jeff is also Director for Saskatchewan at Clean Prosperity, a Canadian climate and energy policy organization. He does not edit Eye Today coverage of that organization or its work.

Eye Today is owned by EvolveX360, a digital media and marketing company based in Durban, South Africa, whose team supports the newsroom day to day under the editor's direction.
