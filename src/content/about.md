Eye Today is an independent newsroom covering ibogaine, psychedelic medicine, and the people, policy and science around them.

We write for readers who want sourced reporting rather than hype. Stories are reported and edited by people. Advertising and sponsorship do not decide what we cover. The [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) says how that works, and the [disclaimer](/disclaimer) says what this site is not.

## Who publishes Eye Today

Eye Today is published by **EvolveX360**, a digital-marketing and media company based in Durban, South Africa, which also owns the technology the site runs on. The Editor and Publisher is **Jeff Davis**, a journalist who has reported for the National Post, the Globe and Mail and the Canadian Press, and who sets what we cover and what we print. Day-to-day production is handled by the EvolveX360 team under his editorial direction.

EvolveX360 is a marketing company, and some of its clients are treatment providers. That relationship is disclosed on every page where it matters: on the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy), on any directory listing for a client, and in any story where a client is named. No client, advertiser or supporter sees a story before it runs or has a say in what we publish.

The newsroom can be reached at [newsroom@theeye.today](mailto:newsroom@theeye.today).
