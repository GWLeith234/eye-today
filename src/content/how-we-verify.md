This page is a draft for editorial review. It is not a clinical endorsement, and it is not legal advice.

Eye Today lists organisations so readers can see what each one says it is, and what we have actually checked. A listing is not a recommendation. We do not sell placement, and we do not publish star ratings or reader reviews.

## Listed

The name and the public contact details are on the page. An editor has not confirmed them. Treat a listed entry as a pointer, not as a check.

## Verified

An editor has looked at the organisation's public website and contact details and written a note saying what they looked at. Verified means we could find the organisation. It does not mean we endorse the service, and it does not mean a treatment works.

## Medically supervised

This level is for an editor to use only when a named, licensed clinician is responsible for the medical care, and the editor has recorded who that is and where they are licensed. Until the publisher adopts this draft, treat the level as unused. It is not a statement that a treatment is safe, appropriate or legal.

## What we do not do

- We do not rate listings and we do not publish reader reviews.
- We do not take payment for a higher place in the list. With no search words, the list is alphabetical. With a search, it follows the words in the name, description, city and services.
- We do not write country legal summaries for you. Those pages are written by an editor, with sources and an "as of" date, or they stay unpublished.

## Before you travel or start treatment

Laws and clinical practice change. Read the country page, follow its sources, and talk to a clinician who can advise you. The note at the bottom of every directory page is the standing warning.
