This page is a draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It may change.

Comments on Eye Today are for readers who are signed in. They are plain text. Every new comment is read by an editor, or by an editor's tools, before it appears.

## What belongs here

- Respond to what the story says. Disagree if you like, and say why.
- Share your own experience in your own words, if it helps other readers understand the story.
- Be kind to the people who write and comment here.

## What we remove

- **Personal medical advice.** Please do not ask for it and do not give it. We are not able to tell you what is safe for you. Talk to a doctor.
- **Dosing.** Amounts, schedules, combinations and how-to instructions for taking any substance.
- **Sourcing.** Where to buy, make or grow a substance, or who sells it.
- **Abuse.** Harassment, hate, threats, spam and advertising.

## What happens to a comment

A comment that is held for review is visible to you, marked as held. An editor may publish it, or reject it and say why. An editor may also remove a comment after it appears, and may limit or end an account's ability to comment.

If you see a comment that breaks these guidelines, use Report under it.
